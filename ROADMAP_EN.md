# PAWSON AI Public Roadmap

[简体中文](ROADMAP.md) · **English**

This roadmap changes as evidence develops. It is not a release-date commitment.

## Current state: P0 has produced partial conclusions

- The isolated runtime probe finished with 15 of 16 cases passing.
- Basic conversation, tool loops, identity fields, and most failure cases now have repeatable evidence.
- The upstream connection did not close promptly after user cancellation; this remains a known limitation.
- Real gateway protocol behavior and provider-side metering have not yet been accepted.

## Next phase

- Complete subscription, authorization, request-level pricing, usage, and audit boundaries.
- Improve recovery for long tasks and interrupted connections.
- Develop organization, member, device, and multi-account management.
- Continue improving installation, upgrades, and runtime status feedback.

## Long-term direction

- Connect business data, product projects, knowledge assets, and agent collaboration into one workflow.
- Expand maintained experts, Skills, and external connectors.
- Preserve local-first storage, human approval, traceable evidence, and recoverable failures.
- Turn more analysis and production results into verifiable, reusable deliverables.

## What the roadmap will not announce early

- Model availability that has not passed live-service verification.
- Production back-end capabilities that have not completed migration.
- Features that exist only as UI or plans without executable outcomes.
