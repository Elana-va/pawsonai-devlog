# August 26, 2026: Connecting Collection, Data, and Product Projects

[简体中文](2026-08-26.md) · **English**

## The earlier problem

Collected product images and information often lived only inside one task. Business analysis, the knowledge base, and product-detail pages could not reliably reuse the same material.

## What was completed

- Brought the data collection center into the product interface.
- Turned product candidates, trends, and channel information into readable product capabilities.
- Allowed collected results to be registered as data assets with provenance.
- Enabled the knowledge base to receive data assets and manage their access scope.
- Began shaping product details into independent, editable project workspaces.

## How the roadmap changed

The product moved from “collection is the finish line” to “collection begins the data lifecycle.” Source, processing state, and intended use must remain available so the Agent can obtain trusted context in future tasks.
