# July 25, 2026: The First Deliverable Windows Build

[简体中文](2026-07-25.md) · **English**

## The problem we needed to solve

Producing product images required constant switching among generation, background removal, cropping, upscaling, and template previews. The early goal was to complete one end-to-end workflow in the Windows client and produce real files before expanding to every scenario.

## Milestones completed

- Produced installer and portable distributions.
- Connected text-to-image, reference-image generation, local background removal, precision cropping, image upscaling, and template previews.
- Established task persistence, human review points, and safe export boundaries.
- Completed 51 test files and 152 automated tests.

## What we learned

An AI response that says “success” does not prove the business task is complete. Output files, dimensions, formats, and task states must be independently verifiable. A failure must also preserve the user's existing material and valid results.

## What remained unresolved

External generation quality and model availability still depended on real service environments. The installer also did not yet have commercial code signing. These limits were recorded explicitly in the delivery notes.
