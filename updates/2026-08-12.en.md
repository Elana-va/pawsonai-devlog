# August 12, 2026: Reports Became Real Deliverables

[简体中文](2026-08-12.md) · **English**

## Background

Business analysis that exists only as a chat response is hard to review, share, or act on. PAWSON needed to turn conclusions, evidence, and recommended actions into deliverable content.

## Changes in this stage

- Improved the report center's reading and delivery experience.
- Connected report output paths for enterprise collaboration tools.
- Defined clearer management boundaries for cross-border task artifacts.
- Saved stable checkpoints for later product-detail and Agent development.

## Product judgment

A reliable report needs more than complete prose. It must preserve metric definitions, sources, missing information, and next actions. The expert system and document delivery features continued to follow this requirement.
