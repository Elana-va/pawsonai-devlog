# August 4, 2026: Closing the Cross-Border Workflow Offline First

[简体中文](2026-08-04.md) · **English**

## The goal

We separated product-data preparation, title generation, and platform operations into independently verifiable modules. This allowed the code and UI workflow to close before a real store account was available.

## What was completed

- Established the foundation for three cross-border business modules.
- Verified offline data, task states, and interface interactions.
- Completed 190 test files and 855 tests without additional parallel load.
- Marked draft saving and real publishing as requiring manual acceptance with a platform test account.

## Why we did not claim the platform was connected

Third-party pages, login states, selectors, and risk-control rules can change. Offline tests prove the product's internal workflow; they do not prove that a real store operation still works that day. From this stage onward, we consistently separated code completion, offline acceptance, and real business acceptance.
