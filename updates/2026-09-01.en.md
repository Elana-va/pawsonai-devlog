## Boundaries that had to hold

A command is not automatically safe because an Agent proposed it. Reading, writing, sending, and publishing require different permissions, and sensitive actions wait for user approval. Generated files must also be verified on disk; placeholders or stale cache entries cannot stand in for successful delivery.

## Why this mattered

The Agent gained the execution and delivery capabilities needed to finish work while keeping approval, recovery, and evidence ahead of capability expansion.
